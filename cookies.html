Digital Stewardship

Cookie Policy & Digital Preferences

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•JacketDistrict Privacy Standards

1. Principles of Digital Elegance and Client Privacy

JacketDistrict upholds an unwavering commitment to digital elegance, distraction-free browsing, and client data privacy. We believe your exploration of our fine leathercraft and outerwear archives should remain private and unburdened by invasive third-party ad retargeting trackers. This Cookie Policy transparently details our use of essential session storage and local preferences.

2. Strictly Necessary Technical & Session Cookies

Essential technical cookies are required for the secure operation, cryptographic verification, and rendering stability of our platform. These cookies maintain encrypted SSL/TLS session states, verify client commission form submissions against cross-site request forgery, and calculate reading progress percentages across journal articles. Essential cookies cannot be disabled without impairing platform functionality.

3. Local Storage for Palette and Configurator Preferences

To ensure optimal visual comfort during extended reading of our technical leather treatises, JacketDistrict utilizes client-side HTML5 Local Storage to remember your preferred interface theme (Foundry Dark vs. Atelier Light) and last-used Jacket Workbench selections. This configuration data resides strictly within your local browser sandbox and is never transmitted to marketing servers.

4. Anonymized Measurement & Performance Analytics

We deploy privacy-compliant Google Analytics 4 telemetry (Google Tag `G-0LY0HY7L01`) to assess aggregate website loading latencies, device viewport dimensions, and navigational engagement. All visitor IP addresses are automatically pseudonymized at the point of ingestion, ensuring no individual client identity is ever associated with website visits.

5. Browser Management and Cookie Controls

Clients maintain complete freedom to review, disable, or delete stored cookies and local storage tokens at any time through web browser preferences (supported in Safari, Chrome, Firefox, and Edge). Please note that clearing essential storage may reset your custom theme preferences.

6. Governance Inquiries and Data Stewardship Contact

For questions regarding our cookie protocols or to discuss our digital privacy governance, please contact our data stewardship desk at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone us at +1-888-777-5845.
