Client Stewardship

Privacy Policy & Client Data Confidentiality

Effective Date: January 1, 2026•Outerwear Atelier Privacy Standards

1. Commitment to Client Discretion and Data Privacy

At JacketDistrict Outerwear Foundry, we hold the privacy, personal discretion, and transaction confidentiality of our bespoke commission clients and digital visitors in the highest regard. We operate under strict data minimization principles, adhering fully to the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and international privacy governance frameworks to ensure your fitting measurements and order records remain completely secure.

2. Scope and Nature of Information Collected

To coordinate private atelier appointments, execute bespoke outerwear commissions, and fulfill deliveries, we collect contact details (such as full name, private email, telephone number, anatomical chest measurements, and delivery addresses). Technical browsing telemetry, including pseudonymized IP addresses and device browser parameters, is gathered exclusively to maintain secure encrypted connections and optimize platform rendering performance.

3. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Processing

Personal data is processed strictly to fulfill bespoke jacket commissions, coordinate private Mercer Street salon fittings, dispatch requested leathercraft garments, and respond to collector inquiries. We never utilize client data for automated behavioral profiling, intrusive tracking networks, or unsolicited commercial marketing campaigns.

4. Absolute Guarantee Against Commercial Data Sale

JacketDistrict enforces an absolute institutional guarantee: we never sell, lease, monetize, or publicly disclose client records or purchase histories to third-party marketing syndicates, ad networks, or data brokers. Information is shared solely with essential operational infrastructure providers—such as secure cloud servers and SSL-encrypted courier gateways—under strict non-disclosure obligations.

5. Cryptographic Security Architecture and Data Retention

All digital communications and fitting requests are protected using modern TLS 1.3 encryption protocols during transit and AES-256 military-grade encryption at rest. Client sizing records and bespoke jacket serial archives are maintained securely within isolated databases, accessible solely by authorized atelier personnel.

6. Universal Client Privacy Rights and Contact

Clients maintain full legal rights to review, update, export, or request the immediate erasure of their personal records at any time. To exercise your privacy rights or discuss our data stewardship protocols, please contact our Data Governance Officer at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone our concierge desk at +1-888-777-5845.
