2. Bespoke Commission Cycles and Creation Schedules

Because each JacketDistrict garment is individually hand-cut from 3.5 oz vegetable-retanned horsehide or dense Scottish waxed canvas, production requires 6 to 10 weeks from initial hide selection to final inspection. Custom hardware plating, personalized leather embossing, or custom shearling collars require an additional 2 weeks of dedicated benchwork.

4. Natural Hide Characteristics and Patina Evolution

Full-grain vegetable-tanned horsehide, natural shearling fleece, and waxed cotton are organic natural materials. Subtle grain variations, minor range marks, natural scars, and dynamic tea-core fading are authentic hallmarks of uncorrected luxury hides and do not constitute manufacturing defects.

Because bespoke jackets are cut to individual client measurements, custom commissions are non-refundable once hide cutting has commenced.
