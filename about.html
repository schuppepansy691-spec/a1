The Uncompromising Art of Heavyweight Outerwear

By JacketDistrict Master Leathercrafters•Atelier Standards
Inside our Mercer Street cutting atelier: where vegetable-tanned horsehide hides and Scottish waxed canvas are tailored into heirloom outerwear.

In a contemporary garment industry plagued by fast-fashion polyurethane composites and bonded leather compromises, JacketDistrict stands as an unyielding fortress of authentic heavyweight outerwear craftsmanship. We believe a jacket should not merely survive a season; it should serve as a generational heirloom that grows more beautiful, supple, and character-rich with every passing decade.

Our Three Immutable Atelier Standards

1. Full-Grain Integrity: We never use corrected-grain, sanded, or split leathers. Every natural scar, grain variation, and oil pull-up characteristic of the animal hide is preserved.
2. Solid Metallurgical Hardware: Our zippers and snaps are forged from solid antiqued brass and gunmetal alloys (such as Italian Raccagni Super R zippers), engineered to withstand decades of rigorous operation.
3. Anatomical Articulation: Leather jackets are sculpted with bi-swing action backs, articulated sleeve curves, and drop-tail hems, ensuring total freedom of movement whether riding a motorcycle or walking through winter gales.
